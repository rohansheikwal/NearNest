Pricing & Payments

No. Neighborly does not process payments. The pricing listed is a starting figure provided by the provider. Payment arrangements are made directly between the customer and provider outside the platform.
The price shown is the provider's listed rate as a starting point — per hour, per visit, or per project. The actual cost for your specific work may vary depending on the complexity and time required. We encourage discussing details with the provider before committing.

Reviews

Only customers whose service request has been marked as Completed can leave a review. Each completed request allows one review. Ratings are calculated from real reviews — no artificial or default ratings are added.
In this version, providers cannot formally respond to reviews. Administrators can moderate inappropriate content through the admin panel.
