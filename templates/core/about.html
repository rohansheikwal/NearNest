About Neighborly

Local Services, Stronger Communities

What Is Neighborly?

Neighborly is a community-focused platform that connects people who need everyday home and local services with skilled individuals who live in the same city or neighbourhood.

Finding a reliable local plumber, cleaner, or tutor is harder than it should be. People often rely on word-of-mouth referrals — information that isn't always accessible or organised. Neighborly brings that trust and local knowledge into a structured, searchable platform that anyone can use.

This project was designed and built as a BTech Computer Science final-year application, with the aim of creating a genuinely functional, well-designed service marketplace using Django. Every feature on this platform — from search and filtering to service requests and review workflows — is implemented with a real database and server-side logic.
