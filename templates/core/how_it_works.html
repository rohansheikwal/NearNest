How Neighborly Works

Simple steps for customers and providers.

For Customers

Finding reliable local services in five easy steps.

1
Create a Free Account

Register as a customer with your name, city, and email. It takes under a minute.

2
Find a Service

Use the search bar and filters — by keyword, category, city, area, or price — to discover providers near you.

3
Review Provider Profiles

Read service descriptions, see pricing, check availability, and review ratings from previous customers.

4
Submit a Request

Click "Request This Service," fill in your preferred date, time, location, and description of the work needed.

5
Track & Review

Follow your request from Pending to Completed on your dashboard. Leave a genuine review once the service is done.

Get Started as Customer

For Providers

Start reaching local customers with your skills.

1
Register as a Provider

Create a provider account with your skills, city, and a short bio about your experience.

2
List Your Services

Create service listings with a clear title, description, your pricing, availability, and a service photo.

3
Receive Requests

When a customer sends a request, it appears on your Provider Dashboard with all their details.

4
Manage Your Work

Accept or reject requests. Mark accepted requests as In Progress, then Completed when the job is done.

5
Build Your Reputation

Satisfied customers leave genuine reviews. Your ratings appear on your profile and services to attract more work.
