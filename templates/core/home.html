Quick answers to common questions about Neighborly.

You can browse and view service listings without an account. However, you'll need to register (for free) as a customer to submit a service request or save services.
Find a service you need, click "View Details," and then click "Request This Service." Fill in your preferred date, time, location, and a description of the work needed.
Yes — customers can cancel requests that are still in Pending or Accepted status. Once a request is In Progress or Completed, it cannot be cancelled.
