Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

At NearNest, we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal data you share with us. This policy describes how your information is handled across our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you register on NearNest, we collect: your name, email address, username, city, area, and optionally a phone number and profile photo. If you are a provider, we also collect your skills and service descriptions. Service requests include location information, work descriptions, and dates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: allow you to create and manage your account, connect customers with providers, display your profile and services to other users, and enable the service request and review workflow. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Information Visibility

Provider profiles (name, city, area, bio, skills) are publicly visible. Customer names appear in reviews but email addresses, phone numbers, and exact home addresses are never publicly displayed. Service request location details are only shared with the relevant provider.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account information is stored securely on encrypted database infrastructure. Uploaded media files and profiles are protected with strict access controls. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text.

5. Cookies and Sessions

NearNest uses secure session cookies to keep you safely logged in and protect against cross-site request forgery. No unauthorized tracking or third-party advertising cookies are used.

6. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or your data, please use the contact form.
